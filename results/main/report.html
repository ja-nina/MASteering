Steering report

Persona-vector steering of LLM agents in multi-agent games: does it change what they say, how they play, and what happens to the group?

Key findings
  • 8 of 8 traits move significantly in the steered direction. Strongest: empathy (+11.75), assertive (+9.15), sycophantic (+5.82); weakest: evil (+1.66), agreeableness (+1.97), trustworthiness (+2.42) SD per unit coefficient.
  • 56 of 56 off-diagonal cells are significant: the persona vectors are strongly entangled (they share a common 'persona' axis), so a trait readout alone does not tell which trait was steered.
  • Average AUC 0.88 (0.5 = chance). A readout monitor singles out the steered agent in most episodes.
  • Prompt vs vector: for 5 of 16 trait × side cases the prompt's effect on the readout equals the vector's somewhere in the tested range (median |c| ≈ 1.3); the prompt goes beyond the strongest vector for agreeableness +, agreeableness −, assertive +, empathy −, evil +, sarcastic +, sycophantic +, trustworthiness −.
  • Group welfare, significant: evil_team × sycophantic: -0.43; evil_team × agreeableness: -0.42; evil_team × assertive: -0.41; evil_one × agreeableness: -0.21; all × agreeableness: -0.13; half × agreeableness: -0.10.
  • The unsteered co-players pick up the trait for: empathy (half): +0.38; sarcastic (half): +0.36; empathy (both_one): +0.33; agreeableness (both_one): +0.30; sarcastic (both_one): +0.28; trustworthiness (both_one): +0.25.
  • Pooled over games, the steered agent's reward changes for: agreeableness: -0.15; assertive: +0.09; opportunistic: -0.07; sarcastic: +0.04; empathy: -0.04.
  • Steering the whole deceiving side changes its win rate for: agreeableness: +0.33; assertive: +0.30; sycophantic: +0.30; sarcastic: -0.12 (positive = the deceivers win more).
  • Steering changes the outcome for: agreeableness, assertive, empathy, opportunistic, sarcastic; the readout predicts the outcome for: agreeableness, assertive, empathy, opportunistic, sycophantic.
  • Steering moves the language more when the agent sees an image, for 7 of 8 traits (assertive, empathy, evil, opportunistic, sarcastic, sycophantic, trustworthiness).
